# Sources — Wednesday 18 November 2026

Every story on the front page was verified against reporting from 18 November
2026 (events that happened, or were first reported, on that day). No figure,
quote or casualty number is invented.

## Lead — Three leaders meet in Shenzhen

- Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin met in Shenzhen on Wednesday, a day before a
  first-ever three-way summit with Donald Trump (The Hindu; BBC, via Level
  Headlines daily brief, 18 Nov 2026).
- Trump landed in Shenzhen and was personally greeted by Xi on the tarmac
  (ABC News, via Algenom, 18 Nov 2026). APEC opens in Shenzhen.
- The US–China tariff truce runs to 10 January 2027 (Al Jazeera, Fortune).

## The Gulf — US strikes Iran site; Tehran hits back

- US fighter jets struck a Revolutionary Guard missile launch site on Iran's
  Qeshm Island in response to Iranian attacks on US personnel and assets
  (Reuters and AFP, via Algenom, 18 Nov 2026).
- Iran said it targeted US drone and missile sites in Kuwait overnight, and
  fired at Bahrain (Reuters, via Level Headlines, 18 Nov 2026).

## Ukraine — Kerch Bridge road span collapses

- Ukrainian forces struck the Kerch Strait Bridge for a second consecutive
  day; the automobile sections completely collapsed and the railway section
  was damaged (Institute for the Study of War, Russian Offensive Campaign
  Assessment, 17 Nov 2026; reported 18 Nov 2026).
- Ukraine's General Staff: the road section is completely out of service;
  the railway section remained standing, with traffic partly resumed.
- Crimea's Russian-installed governor Sergei Aksyonov: two people killed,
  road traffic suspended (via NY Post / EuropeSays, 18 Nov 2026).

## US midterms — Peltola wins Alaska; Senate 56–44

- Democrat Mary Peltola defeated two-term Republican Sen. Dan Sullivan in
  Alaska, the last US Senate race of the midterms to be resolved (Associated
  Press, via Level Headlines, 18 Nov 2026), giving Democrats a 56–44
  majority.

## Tech — Nvidia posts a record $115.8bn quarter

- Nvidia reported fiscal third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103%
  year over year, beating the ~$109.1 billion consensus; data-centre revenue
  $107.6 billion (CNBC, Motley Fool earnings-call transcript, 18 Nov 2026).
- First earnings report since Jensen Huang resigned as CEO on 12 November;
  interim CEO Colette Kress led the call; the board said it does not intend
  to reinstate Huang as CEO (CNBC, 18 Nov 2026).

## Health — Ebola outbreak passes 11,000 cases

- The Ebola outbreak in DR Congo has more than doubled in three months,
  with over 11,000 cases and 5,400 deaths (United Nations, via One Turn /
  UN News, 18 Nov 2026).

## Weather — Cyclone Akvan bears down on India

- Cyclone Akvan strengthens in the Bay of Bengal; the India Meteorological
  Department forecasts landfall on India's Andhra Pradesh coast around noon
  on Friday 20 November as a very severe cyclonic storm, with sustained
  winds of 120–130 km/h gusting to 145 km/h (The Watchers, Swarajya, via
  Algenom, 18 Nov 2026).
